## Business Scenario

A manufacturing company wants to identify which product defects should be fixed first.

---

|-----------|-------------|------:|
| D01 | Scratch | 78 |

| D05 | Loose Part | 20 |
| D06 | Crack | 15 |
| D07 | Missing Label | 12 |
| D08 | Other | 5 |

---

## Questions

### Question 1

Sort the defects in descending order based on Count.

---

### Question 2

Calculate:

- Total Count
- Percentage (%)

---

### Question 3

Calculate:

- Cumulative Count
- Cumulative Percentage

---

### Question 4

Create a Pareto Chart using:

- Column Chart → Count
- Line Chart → Cumulative Percentage

### Question 5

## Questions

### Question 1

Calculate the following values using Excel formulas:

- Q1
- Median

---

### Question 2

Calculate the Interquartile Range (IQR).

IQR = Q3 − Q1

---

### Question 3

Calculate:

- Lower Fence
- Upper Fence

Lower Fence = Q1 − (1.5 × IQR)

Upper Fence = Q3 + (1.5 × IQR)
