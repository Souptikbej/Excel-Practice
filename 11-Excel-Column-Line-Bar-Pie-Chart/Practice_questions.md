# 🎤 Interview Thinking

Be prepared to explain the following concepts in an interview.

---

## 1. Why would you use a Column Chart?

A Column Chart makes it easy to **compare numerical values across discrete categories**.

**Example:**

> Comparing annual sales between Laptop, Mobile, Tablet, and Accessories.

---

## 2. Why use a Line Chart?

A Line Chart is effective for showing **movement, trends, and patterns across an ordered time period**.

**Example:**

> Analyzing monthly sales from January to December.

---

## 3. Why use a Pie Chart?

A Pie Chart is useful when the objective is to show **how categories contribute to a whole**, especially when there are only a few categories.

**Example:**

> Showing the percentage contribution of each product to total annual sales.

---

## 4. What does a Linear Trendline show?

A Linear Trendline summarizes the **overall direction of a relationship or time series using a straight-line model**.

It helps identify whether the data is generally:

* Increasing
* Decreasing
* Relatively stable

---

## 5. What does R² represent?

**R² (R-squared)** indicates how well the linear trendline explains the variation in the observed data.

In general:

* **R² closer to 1** → stronger linear fit
* **R² closer to 0** → weaker linear fit

> ⚠️ A high R² does not automatically mean the model is appropriate or that the forecast will be accurate.

---

## 6. What does a Moving Average do?

A Moving Average **smooths short-term fluctuations** by calculating the average value over a specified number of periods.

For example, a **3-month moving average** considers three consecutive months at a time.

It can help management identify the **underlying direction of sales** without being distracted by individual monthly spikes or drops.
