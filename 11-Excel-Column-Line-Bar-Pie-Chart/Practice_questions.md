# Problem 1 --- Column Chart: Compare Product Sales

## Business Requirement

Management wants to compare the **total annual sales generated by each
product**.

## Task

Create a **Column Chart** showing:

-   Laptop
-   Mobile
-   Tablet
-   Accessories

against their **total Sales (₹)**.

## Requirements

1.  Summarize total Sales by Product.
2.  Select the summarized data.
3.  Insert a Column Chart.
4.  Add a meaningful chart title.
5.  Add appropriate data labels.
6.  Format the Sales axis using ₹ where appropriate.

### Suggested Chart Title

**Annual Sales by Product**

## Business Questions

1.  Which product generated the highest total sales?
2.  Which product generated the lowest total sales?
3.  How large is the difference between the highest- and lowest-selling
    products?
4.  Why is a Column Chart suitable for this comparison?

# 💡 Interview Thinking

Be prepared to explain these questions:

### 1. Why would you use a Column Chart?

Because it makes it easy to compare values across discrete categories.

### 2. Why use a Line Chart?

Because it is effective for showing movement and patterns over an
ordered time period.

### 3. Why use a Pie Chart?

When the objective is to show how categories contribute to a whole and
the number of categories is limited.

### 4. What does a Linear Trendline show?

It summarizes the overall direction of a time-series relationship using
a straight-line model.

### 5. What does R² represent?

R² indicates how well the linear trendline explains the variation in the
observed data. A value closer to 1 indicates a stronger linear fit.

### 6. What does a Moving Average do?

It smooths short-term fluctuations by averaging values over a specified
number of periods.
